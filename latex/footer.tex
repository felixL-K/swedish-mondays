Do not forget to ask questions, offer your opinion or ask for help if you don't understand! Here are some phrases you can use:

\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
    \item \swedishg{Jag håller (inte) med dig.}{I (don't) agree with you.}
    \item \swedishg{Det förstår jag.}{That's understandable.}
    \item \swedishg{Vad intressant!}{How interesting!}
    \item \swedishg{Vad betyder \placeholder?}{What does \placeholder mean?}
    \item \swedishg{Hur säger man \placeholder på svenska?}{How does one say \placeholder in Swedish?}
    \item \swedishg{Kan du upprepa lite långsammare?}{Can you repeat a bit slower?}
    \item \swedishg{Nu förstår jag!}{I understand now!}
\end{itemize}
}
