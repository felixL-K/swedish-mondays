Do not forget to ask questions and offer your opinion! Here are some phrases you can use to contribute when someone else is talking:
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
    \item \swedishg{Jag håller (inte) med dig.}{I (don't) agree with you.}
    \item \swedishg{Det förstår jag.}{That's understandable.}
    \item \swedishg{Varför tycker/gjorde du så?}{Why do you think/did you do that?}
    \item \swedishg{Du sa att \placeholder, kan du utveckla det?}{You said that \placeholder, can you elaborate on it?}
    \item \swedishg{Å ena sidan \placeholder, å andra sidan \placeholder}{On the one hand \placeholder, on the other hand \placeholder}
    \item \swedishg{Vad intressant!}{How interesting!}
\end{itemize}

If you have trouble following the conversation, here are some phrases you can use to ask for help:
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
    \item \swedishg{Vad betyder \placeholder?}{What does \placeholder mean?}
    \item \swedishg{Hur säger man \placeholder på svenska?}{How does one say \placeholder in Swedish?}
    \item \swedishg{Kan du upprepa lite långsammare?}{Can you repeat a bit slower?}
    \item \swedishg{Jag förstår inte vad du menar med \placeholder}{I don't understand what you mean by \placeholder}
    \item \swedishg{Nu förstår jag!}{I understand now!}
\end{itemize}
