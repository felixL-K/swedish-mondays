Swedish Mondays manifesto:

\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
    \item Our primary focus is speaking and conversation. Try to keep the chat going and to include everyone by asking questions. If you don't understand something, ask away!
    \item Try to say what you want to say, even if it's difficult. Our Swedish volunteers will help you express yourself if you get stuck.
    \item The topics are simple guidelines for inspiration and getting the conversation started. It's okay to diverge or skip them entirely.
\end{itemize}
